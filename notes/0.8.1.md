- **Type straight into the pages.** Click the words of a text item in the pages on the right, and you can type there, as well as in the box in the middle. The pages catch up when you click somewhere else.
- **Line spacing for the whole bulletin.** Under **Design**, set how far apart the lines are, for all the text at once. Any item can still have its own spacing, where you edit it, and headings and hymn titles now have that too.
- **Blank lines and spaces at the start or end are kept.** Before, they disappeared as you typed. Now a blank line or two at the end of a text item is an easy way to move what follows it down a little.

To install it fresh, see [which file to download](https://github.com/aubrika/grammateus-releases#which-file-to-download).
